A savings club or investment group, adopted on

1. Name and purpose

  1. The group is called (“the group”).
  2. The group is a savings club that saves towards a payout   an investment group that grows its money together   both.
  3. The group saves for: .
  4. The group is not run for the profit of any one member. Its money belongs to the group and is shared between members only as this constitution sets out.

2. Members

  1. A person becomes a member when they are invited by the chairperson, secretary or treasurer and they themselves accept the invitation. Nobody is made a member without agreeing to it.
  2. The group will have no more than members.
  3. Every member agrees that every other member may see what they have contributed, what they owe and what their share of the group’s money is.

3. Office-bearers

  1. The group has a chairperson, a treasurer and a secretary, elected at the annual general meeting for year(s). They may be re-elected.
  2. The chairperson runs meetings. The treasurer keeps track of contributions and the group’s money. The secretary keeps the minutes and the list of members.
  3. Holding an office does not, on its own, give anybody the right to approve a payment. Only signatories approve payments (section 5).

4. Contributions

  1. Each member contributes R every , due by day of the month. Where members contribute different amounts, the amount for each member is recorded on the account.
  2. A contribution not paid within days of the due date attracts a fine of R. A fine is recorded by the committee with the reason for it, and the member can see it.
  3. Contributions and fines still owed are deducted from a member’s share when they are paid out.

5. The account and the mandate

  1. The group’s money is held in an account in the group’s name with SV Capital. The group, not any one member, is the account holder.
  2. The signatories are: .
  3. No money leaves the account, and none of it is invested, unless signatories have approved that specific amount going to that specific destination. This number is never fewer than two.
  4. No single member, including the chairperson, may move the group’s money alone.
  5. If any signatory rejects a request, the request ends. It may be raised again, but it is not out-voted.
  6. An approval is for one payment only and may not be reused.
  7. A change to who the signatories are, to the number of approvals needed, or to the date the money is locked until, is made by a request that the signatories approve in the same way as a payment. A signatory does not vote on their own removal.

6. Saving and the payout

  1. The group’s money is locked until each year, and nothing is paid out before then except as section 9 allows.
  2. The payout is made on , approved by the signatories as one payment to all members.
  3. The group’s money is shared between members in proportion to what each member has contributed   in equal parts between active members.
  4. Interest and returns credited to the account are shared on the same basis.

7. Investing the group’s money

  1. The group does not invest   may invest, only in line with its investment policy, which forms part of this constitution.
  2. Before any money is invested, the proposal is put to the members, and it is carried only if vote for it. The signatories must then approve that amount into that investment.
  3. Members understand that investments carry risk, that returns are not guaranteed, that capital may be lost in part or in full, and that money in an investment is committed for its full term.
  4. Money the group needs for its payout is not invested in anything that will not have matured by the payout date, unless the members decide otherwise under clause 7.2.

8. Lending

  1. The group does not lend money from its account to members or to anybody else, and the account is not used as security for a loan.

9. Leaving the group

  1. A member may ask to leave at any time. The group may also decide, at a meeting and by , that a member must leave.
  2. A member who leaves is paid their share, less anything they owe the group. Paying them out is a payment from the account and needs the signatories’ approval.
  3. If some of the group’s money is in an investment that has not matured, the member is paid what is available now and the rest when the investment matures. The group does not end an investment early to pay one member.
  4. If a member dies, their share is paid to , subject to the law and to the account provider’s requirements.

10. Meetings and decisions

  1. The group meets , and holds an annual general meeting within months of each year.
  2. A meeting can make decisions when at least members are present. Unless this constitution says otherwise, a decision is made by a majority of the members present.
  3. The secretary keeps minutes of every meeting. At the annual general meeting the treasurer tables the group’s statement.

11. Disputes

  1. A disagreement between members is raised first with the committee, then at a meeting of the group. If it is still not settled, the members may ask to mediate.
  2. The group’s account provider keeps the record of contributions, shares and approvals but does not settle disputes between members.

12. Changing this constitution, and closing the group

  1. This constitution may be changed at a meeting by , with at least days’ notice of the proposed change. The updated constitution is sent to the account provider.
  2. The group may be closed by the same majority. Its money is then paid out to members as their shares, under the mandate, once anything the group owes has been settled.
