Test Investor Statement & IT3(b) Tax Certificate

Follow these steps to trigger the print-window generator from a live allocation record. No login required.

1
Open the Fund Console — click the button below. The page loads without any login.
2
Click "Allocations" in the left sidebar under the Investors nav section.
3
Click the 👁 eye icon on any allocation row — the Allocation Detail modal opens.
4
Scroll to the modal footer and click either:
• 🗒 Investor Statement  or  🏅 IT3(b) Tax Cert
5
A new print window opens. Click "Print / Save PDF" to generate the PDF.
⚠ If a pop-up blocker fires, allow pop-ups for this site and click again.
