3. How much risk we accept

  1. The group’s attitude to risk is cautious   moderate   willing to take more risk for a possibly higher return.
  2. Every member understands that:
    • investments carry risk and capital is at risk: the group may get back less than it put in, or nothing;
    • a target return is not a promise, and returns are not guaranteed;
    • money in an investment is committed for its full term and cannot be withdrawn early;

  3. No more than % of the group’s money is put into any one investment.

  5. The group does not lend money to members or anybody else, and does not invest in a business in which a member has a personal interest unless the members have voted on it knowing of that interest.

5. How a decision is made

  1. Any member may propose an investment, saying how much and into what.
  2. Every member has one vote. Voting stays open for days. The proposal is carried if vote for it.
  3. A carried proposal does not move any money. The signatories must then approve that amount going into that investment, to the number of approvals the constitution requires. Any signatory may reject it, which ends the request.
  4. The 1% platform fee is charged on top of the amount invested, so slightly more than the approved amount leaves the group wallet.
  5. What happens when an investment matures — paid back into the group wallet, or reinvested — is decided when it is approved.

6. Conflicts of interest

  1. A member who would benefit personally from an investment, other than through their share of the group’s money, says so before the vote and does not vote on it.

7. Keeping an eye on it

  1. The treasurer reports at every on what the group has invested, what it has earned and when each investment matures.
  2. The members review this policy at every annual general meeting, and whenever the group’s plans change.
