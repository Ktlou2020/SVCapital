How it goes

Five steps, in this order, every time.

  1. A conversation

    What you are trying to do with the money, over what period, and what you need to see while it is working. Past the entry point nothing else is quoted off a list, because it would be a guess about your situation.

  2. An agreement in writing

    Naming both parties, the pool, the term and the basis the return is calculated on. SmartVest Financial Services (Pty) Ltd is the authorised provider; SV Capital is the fund manager. Nothing is deployed before this is signed.

  3. Allocation to a named pool

    Your capital goes into one pool financing one transaction, not into a general account. Pools are not commingled across strategies.

  4. Reporting while it runs

    A factsheet each reporting period for the pool you are in, and a login that shows your own position. Figures are posted against what the pool did, never projected forward from a rate.

  5. Settlement at the end of the term

    Capital and return, with a statement setting out how each figure was arrived at. Where a strategy distributes along the way, as solar does annually, that is paid as it falls due.

Questions people ask before they commit

Is my capital guaranteed?

No, and anybody who tells you otherwise about an asset-backed pool is selling you something. Capital is at risk. What we can tell you is what secures each pool, who operates it, and what has happened in the pools that have already closed.

How much do I need to place?

R1 000 000. That is the entry point for a private-client relationship — terms agreed with you, reporting to suit, and somebody who answers the phone. Above it, size and structure are still settled in conversation rather than off a price list. Below it you are not turned away: every strategy on this page is open on the main site at its own minimum, which starts a great deal lower.

Can I get out early?

Generally no. A pool is financing a real transaction with a real end date, and the term exists because the cattle have to be finished and the invoice has to be paid. Choose the term deliberately; that is part of the first conversation.

Who is actually responsible for my money?

SmartVest Financial Services (Pty) Ltd, an authorised financial services provider, FSP 52449. SV Capital is the fund manager and administers each pool under that licence. Both are named in your agreement.

What do the rates on the platform mean?

A rate shown before a pool concludes is a target drawn from the underlying trade, lease or venture, and it is labelled as a target. Once a pool closes out, what it actually returned is what is shown.

Do you work through my adviser or accountant?

Yes, including reporting addressed to them. If you allocate for an institution or advise other people’s clients, the institutional page is written for that.
