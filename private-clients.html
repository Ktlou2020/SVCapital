How Our Investment Structure Works

Each allocation follows a defined transaction structure, connecting capital to commercial livestock, an established feedlot operation and an offtake. From deployment to repayment, the process is built around identifiable assets and a defined commercial purpose.

  1. Capital is deployed into commercial livestock

    Capital is allocated to acquire weaner cattle for a commercial feedlot operation. The investment is linked to identifiable livestock managed by an established operating partner, rather than exposure solely to movements in cattle prices.

  2. The livestock serves as collateral

    The herd serves as collateral for the investment, subject to the applicable security agreements. Livestock is insured against specified risks — disease, theft and natural hazards. The operating partner is responsible for veterinary care and biosecurity while the animals are in its custody.

  3. The exit is contracted in advance

    An offtake agreement is established before the investment pool opens, providing a defined route for the cattle to be sold. The arrangement establishes the intended buyer and sale mechanism, although actual realisation and repayment remain subject to the terms of the agreements and the performance of the relevant counterparties.

  4. Repayment follows the investment term

    The minimum investment term is 18 months. During this period, the underlying cattle may pass through several production cycles, each contributing to the commercial activity supporting the transaction. At the end of the agreed term, repayment is determined by the performance of the relevant investment pool. Capital is at risk, and any target return is not guaranteed.

How your investment is structured

A defined process from initial engagement to settlement. We work with private investors, families and their advisers to structure capital allocations around agreed investment objectives, time horizons and reporting requirements.

  1. Understanding your investment objective

    Every engagement begins with a conversation about your investment objectives, capital allocation, investment horizon and reporting requirements. We assess whether the opportunity and its structure align with your requirements before proceeding.

  2. Formal agreements and documentation

    Each allocation is governed by a written agreement setting out the parties, investment structure, agreed term, applicable return methodology and relevant contractual obligations. Documentation is completed before capital is deployed.

  3. Defined capital allocation

    Capital is allocated to a designated pool linked to an underlying cattle transaction. Each pool has its own defined structure and investment terms, with performance determined by the underlying transaction rather than a projected return alone.

  4. Ongoing reporting and transparency

    Investors receive reporting on their respective allocations, providing visibility into the pool’s performance and investment position. Reporting is designed to distinguish actual performance from projections and explain the basis of reported figures.

  5. Settlement and investment outcomes

    At the end of the agreed term, the pool is settled and the investment outcome is determined by its realised performance, in accordance with the governing agreement. Capital and returns are subject to investment risk, and no return is guaranteed.

Understanding the Investment

Is my capital guaranteed?

No. Capital is at risk, and asset-backed investment does not guarantee repayment. The underlying livestock provides security for the arrangement, but investors may still experience losses. We encourage investors to understand the security structure, counterparty arrangements and associated risks before committing capital.

What is the minimum investment?

The minimum allocation is R1 000 000. We engage directly with prospective investors to understand their investment objectives, preferred time horizon and reporting requirements. Terms are agreed and documented before capital is deployed.

How long is my capital committed for?

The minimum investment term is 18 months. Capital is committed for the agreed term, subject to the applicable contractual provisions. The underlying cattle cycle is shorter than the investment term, allowing multiple operating cycles during the allocation period.

How is the investment secured?

The lending structure is supported by the underlying livestock, which serves as security for the relevant transactions. The cattle are insured against specified risks, subject to the applicable policy terms and exclusions. Security arrangements and associated risks should be reviewed in the investment documentation before capital is committed.

Which entities are involved in managing my investment?

SmartVest Financial Services (Pty) Ltd is an authorised financial services provider, FSP 52449, and administers the funds invested. SV Capital is appointed as the alternative fund manager and oversees the fund management and operations of the relevant investment pools under the applicable arrangements. The roles and responsibilities of the parties are documented in the investment agreement.

How should I interpret a quoted investment return?

Any rate quoted before an investment pool concludes is a target based on the anticipated performance of the underlying transactions, not a guaranteed return. Actual returns depend on the results achieved by the relevant pool and the applicable contractual arrangements.

What reporting will I receive?

Investors receive reporting for their relevant investment pool, setting out their position and the performance achieved during the reporting period. Reporting arrangements and frequency are discussed and agreed as part of the investment process.

How is my investment repaid?

Repayment follows the agreed investment terms and the performance of the underlying transactions. The cattle are sold under pre-arranged offtake agreements, with proceeds applied in accordance with the relevant contractual arrangements. Actual outcomes depend on the performance of the investment pool, and repayment is not guaranteed.

Can I invest through my financial adviser or family office?

Yes. We engage with financial advisers, family offices and other professional intermediaries where appropriate. We discuss investment objectives, reporting requirements and mandate considerations to determine whether the opportunity aligns with your requirements. If you allocate for an institution, the institutional page is written for that.
