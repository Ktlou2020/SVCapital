How an allocation is structured

The mechanics are the same whether an allocation comes from an institution, a family office or an individual. What changes is size, reporting cadence and the conversation that sets them.

One pool, one transaction

Capital is allocated to a named pool with a defined underlying asset, a stated term and its own rate. Pools are not commingled across strategies, and a pool's own performance is what settles it.

Licensed and documented

The provider is SmartVest Financial Services (Pty) Ltd, an authorised financial services provider, FSP 52449. SV Capital is the fund manager and administers each pool under that licence. Every allocation is governed by a written agreement naming both parties.

Reported per pool

Each pool carries a factsheet per reporting period and settles into a statement showing capital, return and the basis each figure was arrived at. Figures are posted against the pool, not projected from a rate.

Terms set in conversation

Mandate size, reporting cadence, co-investment and any structure specific to your committee are agreed before anything is deployed. We would rather decline an allocation than fit it to a product that does not suit it.

Capital is at risk

No pool guarantees a return. A rate shown before a pool concludes is a target drawn from the underlying trade, lease or venture, and is presented as one.
