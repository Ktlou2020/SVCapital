The Cattle Investment, in detail

The oldest line we run, and the one diligence spends the most time on. Set out in full here because the mechanics answer most of the questions that follow: what the capital buys, who holds it, what secures it, and what has to happen before a return is earned.

The capital buys a herd

Pooled capital is deployed into weaners bought into a commercial feedlot. These are real, identifiable animals under a named operator, not an exposure to a cattle price index.

Backgrounding at Beefcor

Around sixty days of conditioning — feeding, health management and preparation for the feedlot — at Beefcor, one of the largest feedlot operators in the country, under veterinary oversight and commercial biosecurity standards.

Sold on under an offtake agreement

After backgrounding the cattle are sold to Beefcor for finishing under a pre-negotiated offtake agreement. That contract is the structural feature worth understanding: it settles the price at which the herd leaves the pool, instead of leaving the pool exposed to the market on the day it has to sell.

What secures it

The herd itself. The cattle are insured against disease, theft and natural hazards, and the operator carries the veterinary and biosecurity obligations while they are in its care.

Where the capital sits meanwhile

In the pool opened for that transaction, administered under the SmartVest licence and reported on each period. Pools are not commingled across strategies, and a pool settles on its own performance.

What has to happen for the return

The cattle have to reach weight and the offtake has to be honoured. Capital is at risk. A rate shown before the pool concludes is a target drawn from the underlying trade, not a promise, and what settles the pool is what it actually achieved.

How an allocation is structured

The mechanics are the same whether an allocation comes from an institution, a family office or an adviser’s client. What changes is size, reporting cadence and the conversation that sets them.

One pool, one transaction

Capital is allocated to a named pool with a defined underlying asset, a stated term and its own rate. Pools are not commingled across strategies, and a pool's own performance is what settles it.

Licensed and documented

The provider is SmartVest Financial Services (Pty) Ltd, an authorised financial services provider, FSP 52449. SV Capital is the fund manager and administers each pool under that licence. Every allocation is governed by a written agreement naming both parties.

Reported per pool

Each pool carries a factsheet per reporting period and settles into a statement showing capital, return and the basis each figure was arrived at. Figures are posted against the pool, not projected from a rate.

Terms set in conversation

Mandate size, reporting cadence, co-investment and any structure specific to your committee are agreed before anything is deployed. We would rather decline an allocation than fit it to a product that does not suit it.

Capital is at risk

No pool guarantees a return. A rate shown before a pool concludes is a target drawn from the underlying trade, lease or venture, and is presented as one.
