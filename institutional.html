How the lending works

One asset class, one operating partner, one structure. A facility is opened for a defined transaction, drawn, run to term and settled.

The asset

Weaners bought into a commercial feedlot and fed to slaughter weight. Real, counted animals under a named operator, not an exposure to a cattle price index.

The collateral

The herd itself, insured against disease, theft and natural hazards. The veterinary and biosecurity obligations sit with the operator while the animals are in its care.

The counterparty

Beefcor, one of the largest feedlot businesses in South Africa, which both feeds the herd and contracts to buy it. No facility is opened against a counterparty that has not been through our diligence.

The exit

Contracted in advance. The cattle are sold on to Beefcor for finishing under an offtake agreement negotiated before the facility opens, so the price the herd leaves at is settled rather than taken from the market on the day.

The term

The minimum term is 18 months, and the term for a given allocation is agreed before anything is drawn. The cattle cycle underneath it is short — about 120 days — so an allocation works through several of them before it is repaid.

What has to happen to be repaid

The cattle have to reach weight and the offtake has to be honoured. Capital is at risk. A rate shown before a facility concludes is a target drawn from the underlying trade, not a promise, and a facility settles on what it achieved.

How an allocation is structured

The mechanics are the same whether an allocation comes from an institution, a family office or an adviser’s client. What changes is size, reporting cadence and the conversation that sets them.

One pool, one transaction

Capital is allocated to a named pool against a defined herd, with a stated term and its own rate. Each pool stands alone, and a pool’s own performance is what settles it.

Licensed and documented

The provider is SmartVest Financial Services (Pty) Ltd, an authorised financial services provider, FSP 52449. SV Capital is the fund manager and administers each pool under that licence. Every allocation is governed by a written agreement naming both parties.

Reported per pool

Each pool carries a factsheet per reporting period and settles into a statement showing capital, return and the basis each figure was arrived at. Figures are posted against the pool, not projected from a rate.

Terms set in conversation

Mandate size, reporting cadence, co-investment and any structure specific to your committee are agreed before anything is deployed. We would rather decline an allocation than fit it to something that does not suit it.

Capital is at risk

No pool guarantees a return. A rate shown before a pool concludes is a target drawn from the underlying trade, and is presented as one.
