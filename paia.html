1. Introduction

This manual is published in terms of Section 51 of the Promotion of Access to Information Act, No. 2 of 2000 ("PAIA"). PAIA gives effect to the constitutional right of access to any information held by the State and any information held by another person that is required for the exercise or protection of any rights.

SV Capital (Pty) Ltd ("SV Capital") is committed to transparency and to fulfilling its obligations under PAIA. This manual describes the categories of records held by SV Capital and the process for requesting access to those records.

6. How to Request Access to Records

Any person wishing to access records held by SV Capital must submit a written request using the prescribed PAIA Form C, available from the South African Human Rights Commission or the Department of Justice.

Step 1 — Complete Form C Download and complete the prescribed Form C from the SAHRC website (www.sahrc.org.za). Provide sufficient detail to identify the record(s) requested.
Step 2 — Submit the Request Submit the completed form via email to enquiry@svcapital.co.za with the subject line "PAIA Request — [Your Name]".
Step 3 — Pay the Request Fee A request fee of R35 (as prescribed by regulation) is payable before the request is processed. SV Capital will provide banking details upon receipt of the request. Requesters who can demonstrate that they are unable to pay the fee may apply for exemption.
Step 4 — Await Response SV Capital will respond to your request within 30 days of receipt and payment of the request fee, as required by PAIA. We may seek an extension of up to 30 additional days in appropriate circumstances.

8. Grounds for Refusal

SV Capital may refuse access to records on grounds provided in Chapter 4 of PAIA, including but not limited to:

  • Protection of the privacy of a third party (personal information)
  • Commercial information of third parties (trade secrets, confidential business information)
  • Confidential correspondence with an attorney (legal privilege)
  • Records that could endanger the safety of an individual
  • Records that are publicly available
  • Records the disclosure of which would constitute a breach of a duty of confidence

9. Remedies if Access is Refused

If SV Capital refuses access to a record, the requester may:

  • Apply to the Information Regulator for a review of the decision within 30 days of receiving the refusal
  • Apply to a court for relief, upon exhausting the internal remedies
