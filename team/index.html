EVA Compensation Pool
Economic Value Added — transparent team compensation ledger
Step 1 — Pool Creation
Revenue − Costs = EVA
Gross Revenue minus all Operational Costs forms the Economic Value Added pool for the period.
Step 2 — Split
50% Company / 50% Team
The company retains 50% for reinvestment. The remaining 50% forms the Team Pool distributed to staff.
Step 3 — Distribution
60% Individual / 40% Collective
60% is weighted by KPI score × EVA weight. 40% is split equally across the team as a collective reward.
 Revenue = 2.5% × AUM
