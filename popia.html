1. Introduction

SV Capital (Pty) Ltd ("SV Capital", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA"). This notice explains how we collect, use, store, and share your personal information.

By using our platform or services, you consent to the processing of your personal information as described in this notice.

2. Who We Are

Responsible Party: SV Capital (Pty) Ltd

Registration: South African company

FSP License: #52449 — Authorised Financial Services Provider

3. Personal Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identity information: Full name, South African ID number, date of birth, passport number (if applicable)
  • Contact information: Email address, phone number, residential address
  • Financial information: Bank account details, investment history, wallet balance, transaction records
  • FICA/KYC documents: Certified ID copy, proof of residence, source of funds declarations
  • Employment information: Occupation, employer name
  • Risk profile: Investment risk appetite, financial goals
  • Technical information: IP address, device type, login timestamps

4. Purpose of Processing

We process your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Opening and managing your investment account
  • Complying with Financial Intelligence Centre Act (FICA) Know Your Customer (KYC) obligations
  • Processing investments, deposits, withdrawals, and returns
  • Complying with Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations
  • Sending account statements, tax certificates, and investment notifications
  • Fraud prevention and security monitoring
  • Improving our platform and services
  • Marketing communications (with your consent)

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information on the following grounds:

  • Contractual necessity: To fulfil our obligations under your investment agreement
  • Legal compliance: FICA, FAIS, AML regulations, SARS reporting requirements
  • Legitimate interests: Fraud prevention, security, platform improvement
  • Consent: Marketing communications (you may withdraw consent at any time)

6. Sharing Your Information

We may share your personal information with:

  • Regulators: FSCA, FIC, SARS as required by law
  • Payment processors: Paystack, Ozow (for payment verification)
  • Banking partners: For investment pool management and payouts
  • IT service providers: Cloud hosting and security providers
  • Compliance consultants: For FICA and AML assessments

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

7. Retention of Information

We retain your personal information for the following periods:

  • FICA/KYC documents: 5 years after the end of the business relationship (FICA requirement)
  • Transaction records: 5 years (SARS and FAIS requirements)
  • Account information: Duration of account plus 5 years
  • Marketing preferences: Until you withdraw consent or close your account

8. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Right to object: Object to the processing of your personal information
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time
  • Right to complain: Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive documents
  • Role-based access controls for staff
  • Multi-factor authentication options
  • Regular security audits

10. Cookies and Tracking

Our platform uses cookies and similar technologies for authentication, security, and analytics. By using our platform, you consent to the use of these technologies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, which may affect platform functionality.

11. Changes to This Notice

We may update this POPIA Privacy Notice from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a prominent notice on our platform. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this notice.
