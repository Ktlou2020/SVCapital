FAQ
It depends which money you mean. Your wallet balance is yours to withdraw at any time — request it from the Wallet page and it is paid to your verified bank account. Money already placed in an investment is locked until that investment matures and cannot be released early. When an investment matures its value returns to your wallet, and from there you can withdraw it or invest it again.
A platform fee of 1% of your investment amount is charged once, when you invest. The amount you enter is the total that leaves your wallet — apply R1 010 and R1 000 goes into the pool, R10 is the fee. It appears as a separate fee line on your transaction history. No platform fee is charged on reinvestment — when an investment matures and rolls over, the full matured amount carries forward. Each product also has its own fees, set out in clause 9 of the Terms of Use.
Returns are paid at maturity for cattle and short-term investments. Solar returns are distributed annually.
Go to Maturity Instructions in the sidebar. You must submit before 5:00 PM on your maturity date.
